# Inscope

> Inscope is an AI-powered financial reporting and automation platform for accounting firms and enterprise finance teams. It drafts accurate, GAAP-compliant financial statements, cash flow statements, and audit workpapers in minutes, keeping a human reviewer in the loop to sign off. Inscope is backed by Norwest Venture Partners.
